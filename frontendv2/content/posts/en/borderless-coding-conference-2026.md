On January 17, I was at the **Borderless Coding Conference 2026**, in São Paulo. It was an intense immersion in networking and in strategies for the global software market.

![Pablo Cruz with one of the organizers of the Borderless Coding Conference 2026](/images/blog/pablo-borderless-conference.jpg)

## Engineers who are already out there

What set the event apart was the direct contact with engineers who already work in the international market. It wasn't theory about "someday working abroad": it was people who already do it, telling how they got there.

## Beyond code

Talking about system design and scalability with these people showed me that, to be a global developer, you have to go far beyond code: **communication and solid fundamentals are the foundation of everything**.

![Attendees following a talk](/images/blog/conference-2026-plateia.webp)

I'm heading home with an open mind and focused on applying these lessons to my journey. Let's go! 🚀
